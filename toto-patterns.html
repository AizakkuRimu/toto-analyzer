The Constellation

Every past draw is stretched into a rising chain of 7 numbers (6 winning + the additional number) and broken down into the gaps between them. This page mines those gap chains for the most common runs of ascension, then lets you weave your own 7-star constellation from a single starting number.

Weave a Constellation

Pick — or roll — a first star. The weaver builds a pool of real candidate chains from the ranked ascension patterns and randomized fallbacks, scores each one by gap-run frequency + pair affinity + all 4 shape patterns combined, then weighted-randomly picks among the strongest — so weaving the same number twice won't keep handing back the same 3–4 combos, while still leaning on what the data actually supports. Always lands within 1–49.

Enter or roll a first number, then weave to see the chain.

✨ For fun only — TOTO draws are independent random events. No pattern here can predict the next draw.
